Calculations

H2 Recovery (online) = (Qperm · yperm,online) / (Qfeed · yfeed) × 100 [%]

H2 Recovery (lab) = (Qperm · yperm,lab) / (Qfeed · yfeed,lab) × 100 [%]. Computed only on days with both lab samples.

H2 Purity = yperm [mol %], read directly from the permeate analyzer (online) or sample (lab).

Total / Non-Permeate Ratio = Qfeed / Qnon-perm. Used by the recovery ratio controller. Higher ratio means more H2 being permeated.

Feed Pressure reported in kPag. Site atmospheric pressure default is 93 kPa. Absolute pressure = gauge + 93 kPa.

Alert Tiers

Ratio >= alarm high (default 6.40) raises an alarm: indicates over-recovery, lower the recovery ratio controller setpoint. Online recovery below the recovery min (default 75 %) raises an advisory. Permeate purity below the advisory threshold (default 93 %) raises an advisory; below the alarm threshold (default 90 %) raises an alarm. Feed pressure deviation greater than the configured percent from design (default 5 %) raises an advisory.

Series Conventions

Online series are shown as continuous lines. Lab series are shown as points where samples exist.

Scope

Single-stage hollow-fiber H2 separator, daily aggregated readings. Tracks recovery, purity, feed conditions, and the controller ratio against design and alarm bounds.

Engineering Manual - Membrane Analyzer

1. Purpose & Scope

This dashboard provides daily performance tracking for a single-stage hollow-fiber polymeric H2 membrane separator processing refinery off-gas or hydrogen-rich purge streams. Inputs are aggregated daily from the historian and from spot lab samples. Outputs are recovery, permeate purity, feed conditions, and the controller flow ratio.

The dashboard does not perform design validation. It is a performance monitor for steady-state operation against design references.

2. Equipment Context

Module type: Polymeric hollow-fiber, glassy backbone (typical: polysulfone, polyimide, or polyaramide). Two parallel trains, each with multiple cans (pressure vessels) in series.

Feed: Hydrogen-rich gas, ~87 mol % H2, balance light hydrocarbons (C1 - C5+) plus trace H2S, NH3, N2.

Permeate: H2-enriched product. Design 95.5 mol % H2. Sent to downstream hydrotreating or recycle.

Non-permeate (residue): Hydrocarbon-rich retentate. Sent to fuel gas or further recovery stages.

3. Operating Envelope

Feed pressure: 15.5 - 16.0 MPa gauge (15,500 - 16,000 kPag). Driving force for permeation.

Permeate pressure: ~10.3 MPa gauge. Set by downstream consumer.

Feed temperature: Approximately 90 - 95 °C, controlled to keep above hydrocarbon dew point and below polymer plasticization.

Total feed flow: 85,000 - 90,000 Nm³/hr (Air Products design 96,277 Nm³/hr at 90 % H2 recovery).

Stage cut: ~80 - 85 % at design conditions (high stage cut is consistent with the high-recovery target).

5. Calculations

5.1 H2 recovery (online). Fraction of H2 in the feed reporting to the permeate, computed from continuous analyzer values:

Recovery_online = (q_perm · y_perm_h2_online) / (q_feed · y_feed_h2_online) × 100

Online feed H2 is preferred. If the online feed analyzer is unavailable for a row, the lab feed H2 is used in its place so a recovery value can still be reported.

5.2 H2 recovery (lab). Independent cross-check using lab-only values, computed only when both lab samples are present on the same day:

Recovery_lab = (q_perm · y_perm_h2_lab) / (q_feed · y_feed_h2_lab) × 100

A persistent gap between online and lab recovery ( > 1.5 percentage points ) suggests online analyzer drift or sampling bias and warrants calibration check.

5.3 H2 purity. Direct read of the permeate H2 mole fraction. Design product purity is 95.53 mol %. Online and lab readings shown side by side as a calibration check.

5.4 Total / Non-permeate ratio. The flow ratio that the recovery ratio controller manipulates:

Ratio = q_feed / q_non_perm

Higher ratio means a larger fraction of feed is permeated, i.e. higher recovery. Design value 5.60. The high-ratio alarm at 6.40 indicates over-recovery: the controller is pulling too much through the membrane, which compromises permeate purity and accelerates membrane fouling.

5.5 Stage cut (informational). Although not displayed as a KPI, stage cut θ = q_perm / q_feed is implicit in the ratio and recovery values. At design conditions θ ≈ 0.82.

5.6 Mass balance check. The dashboard backfills q_non_perm as q_feed - q_perm when the historian value is missing. A direct three-flow check is not shown as a KPI but is implicit in the ratio: closure within 1 % is normal.

6. Status Logic

Status is rolled up from individual checks. Order of escalation: alarm overrides advisory overrides OK.

Alarm

  • Total / non-permeate ratio at or above 6.40 (configurable). Action: lower the recovery ratio controller setpoint.
  • Permeate H2 below 90 % (configurable). Action: check for membrane fouling, sweep contamination, or ratio over-pull.

Advisory

  • Online recovery below 75 % (configurable).
  • Permeate H2 below 93 % (configurable).
  • Feed pressure deviating more than 5 % from design (default design 15,890 kPag).

7. Pressure Convention

All pressures are reported in kilopascal gauge (kPag). The site atmospheric pressure default is 93 kPa, configurable in the Data & Configuration panel. Absolute pressure is gauge + atmospheric. For example a feed reading of 15,937 kPag corresponds to 16,030 kPa absolute (16.03 MPa absolute) at the default atmosphere.

8. Series Conventions on Charts

Online series are continuous lines. Lab series are scatter points where samples exist. Design and alarm reference lines are dashed. The chart line breaks at any timestamp where the underlying sensor returns no value, so a flat line is real data and a gap is missing data.

9. Time Range Behavior

The topbar pill bar (1H, 24H, 7D, 30D, 90D) and the inline custom range (number + unit) drive both the trend chart x-axis and the Performance Averages KPI row. The Current Values row is unaffected by the range and always reflects the latest reading in the dataset.

The chart x-axis is padded to the full requested window. If the dataset is shorter than the window, the older portion shows as blank space (the line drops to nothing) rather than being compressed onto a shorter axis.

Window cutoff is computed off the latest dataset timestamp, not off wall-clock time. Demo data and historical CSV uploads behave the same way.

10. Data Quality & Limitations

Lab samples are typically collected every 3 - 5 days. Lab averages over a window are computed only over the lab-sampled days within that window and may reflect a small sample.

The lab and online permeate analyzers measure on slightly different bases (mol % via gas chromatograph vs. vol % via thermal conductivity or NDIR). For ideal-gas refinery streams the difference is well below the noise floor, so the two are compared directly.

The ratio controller variable is a calculated tag in the DCS. The dashboard recomputes it locally from the upstream flow tags, so a small numerical mismatch versus the DCS value is normal (rounding, signal averaging windows).

11. Common Fault Modes

Permeate purity drift down with recovery flat: membrane fouling or partial fiber failure. Check feed for liquid carryover and permeate H2S trend.

Recovery rising with ratio rising: controller over-pulling; expected when feed H2 drops. Tighten ratio setpoint or accept temporary purity loss.

Recovery diverges between online and lab: online feed analyzer drift. Schedule analyzer calibration; in the interim use lab-based recovery for trending.

Feed pressure low: upstream compressor or recycle issue. Recovery and purity will both fall as driving force drops.
