Calculations

Three independent efficiency methods are computed per row and trended over time. Cross-checks between them flag instrument or model drift.

Heat balance (combustion side, headline): ηHB = Qabs / QLHV × 100, where Qabs = QLHV + Qf + Qca - Qs - Qhl. Stoichiometric air is solved from fuel mole fractions and target stack O2.

PTC 4 indirect (loss method): ηPTC4 = 100 - Ldry - Lmoist - Lrad - Lunacc. Each loss expressed as a percentage of fuel LHV on a per-kg-fuel basis.

Process side (cross-check): ηp = Qp / QLHV × 100, where Qp = mp · Cpp · (Tout - Tin).

Status Tiers

Efficiency below 82 % advisory, below 78 % alarm. Stack temperature above 320 C advisory, above 360 C alarm. Bridgewall above 830 C advisory, above 870 C alarm. Stack O2 below 2 % alarm (CO risk), above 6 % advisory (over-air). Excess air above 30 % advisory, above 45 % alarm.

Standards

API 560 (Fired Heaters for General Refinery Service), ASME PTC 4 (Fired Steam Generators - efficiency methodology), API 535 (Burners for Fired Heaters in General Refinery Services), API 538 (Industrial Fired Boilers).

Engineering Manual - Fired Heater Efficiency

1. Purpose & Scope

This dashboard tracks thermal performance of a process-side fired heater on a daily basis. The headline efficiency comes from a combustion-side heat balance using stoichiometric air solved from the fuel composition and target stack O2. Two cross-checks are computed in parallel: an ASME PTC 4 loss-method efficiency from measured stack conditions, and a process-side efficiency from process flow and temperature rise. Persistent gaps between the three methods point to instrument or model drift rather than a real efficiency change.

The page is a performance monitor, not a control system or an SIS. It is not a substitute for a formal ASME PTC 4 acceptance test.

2. Equipment Context

Heater type: Vertical-cylindrical or cabin-box natural-draft refinery process heater, no air preheater. Two bridgewall thermocouples per cell. Common on hot-oil, charge, and reboil services.

Fuel: Refinery fuel gas (methane / hydrogen / C2-C5 blend), composition variable across operating cases. Seven preset blends are bundled (Sheet Reference, Summer EOR, Winter SOR, H2 Rich, C3 Rich, Type Natural Gas startup, Average Fuel Gas) plus custom mole fractions.

Combustion air: Ambient draft, no APH. Air mass flow is computed from fuel composition and target stack O2, not measured directly.

Process fluid: Hot-oil or hydrocarbon stream, single-phase, large mass flow (typical 100 - 200 kg/s), temperature rise 60 - 100 C across the radiant + convection sections.

3. Operating Envelope

Fuel mass flow: 0.6 - 1.0 kg/s (4400 - 7300 Sm3/hr at typical refinery-gas density). Mass flow used directly; volumetric flow shown as a derived quantity.

Stack temperature: 280 - 320 C nominal; design 294 C. Above 360 C indicates fouling on convection bundle or excess air drift.

Bridgewall temperature: 770 - 800 C nominal; advisory at 830 C, alarm at 870 C (tube creep risk above 880 C for typical 9Cr-1Mo metallurgy).

Target stack O2: 4.0 - 4.5 % dry, equivalent to 24 - 27 % excess air. Below 2 % O2 = CO formation risk. Above 6 % = over-air efficiency loss.

Process inlet T: 250 - 270 C; outlet T 330 - 345 C; rise 75 - 85 C across the heater.

Air temperature: Ambient. The negative Q_ca term (air sensible at sub-ambient temperatures relative to Tref 15 C) is normal in winter operation.

5. Calculations

5.1 Fuel LHV from composition. Mass-basis LHV is computed from mole fractions and component LHVs:

MW_avg = Σ zi · MWi

LHV_mass = Σ (zi · MWi / MW_avg) · LHVmass,i

Component values: H2 120.97 MJ/kg, CH4 50.01, C2H6 47.79, C3H8 46.36, n-C4 45.75, i-C4 45.61, n-C5 45.36, i-C5 45.24, C6+ 39.72, CO 10.11. Inerts (N2, CO2, H2O) carry zero LHV. The Sheet Reference Case yields LHV_mass = 49.47 MJ/kg, MW = 14.84 g/mol.

5.2 Stoichiometric air from composition + target O2. For each component the O2 mole requirement and product moles (CO2, H2O) are summed over the fuel basis. Stoichiometric flue-gas moles are then:

n_O2_stoich = Σ zi · O2_per_moli

n_FG_stoich = n_CO2 + n_H2O + n_N2_fuel + n_O2_stoich · (0.79/0.21)

Excess O2 is back-solved from the target stack O2 volume fraction by an algebraic identity:

n_O2_excess = (y_O2,target · n_FG_stoich) / (1 - y_O2,target · (1 + 0.79/0.21))

Total combustion air mass m_ca and stack flue-gas mass m_s follow by adding stoichiometric + excess O2 with the corresponding N2. Stack mass m_s = m_f + m_ca by mass balance.

5.3 Heat balance (combustion side, headline). All flows referenced to Tref = 15 C. Cp values: fuel gas 2.6038, combustion air 1.006, dry flue gas 1.062 kJ/kg/K.

Q_LHV = m_f · LHV_mass · 1000 (kW with m_f in kg/s, LHV in MJ/kg)

Q_f = m_f · Cp_fs · (T_f - T_ref)

Q_ca = m_ca · Cp_ca · (T_ca - T_ref) (negative when T_ca below T_ref)

Q_s = m_s · Cp_fg · (T_s - T_ref)

Q_hl = Q_LHV · radiation_loss%

Q_abs = Q_LHV + Q_f + Q_ca - Q_s - Q_hl

ηHB = Q_abs / Q_LHV × 100 [%]

5.4 Process-side cross-check. Independent of stack measurements:

Q_p = m_p · Cp_p · (T_p,out - T_p,in) (kW)

ηp = Q_p / Q_LHV × 100 [%]

The heat-balance vs process-side gap |Q_abs - Q_p| is the closure check. Less than 2 % of Q_LHV is normal noise. A persistent gap above 5 % indicates fuel-flow drift, process-flow drift, or a wrong Cp_p.

5.5 PTC 4 indirect (loss method). Per ASME PTC 4 §5:

EA = 100 · O2 / (20.95 - O2) (excess air from dry stack O2, App. A)

m_fg,dry = 1 + (1 + EA/100) · A_stoich - m_H2O (kg per kg fuel)

L_dry = m_fg,dry · Cp_fg · (T_s - T_amb) / LHV_kJ_kg × 100 (pp)

L_moist = m_H2O · Cp_v · (T_s - T_f) / LHV_kJ_kg × 100 (pp, sensible only on LHV basis)

ηPTC4 = 100 - L_dry - L_moist - L_rad - L_unacc

L_rad and L_unacc are configured percentages (defaults 1.5 % and 0.5 %).

6. Status Logic

Status is rolled up from per-row checks. Order of escalation: alarm overrides advisory overrides OK.

Alarm:

  • Heat-balance efficiency below 78 %.
  • Stack temperature above 360 C (convection fouling, severe).
  • Bridgewall temperature above 870 C (tube creep risk).
  • Stack O2 below 2 % (CO formation, incomplete combustion).
  • Excess air above 45 % (severe over-air, energy waste).

Advisory:

  • Heat-balance efficiency below 82 %.
  • Stack temperature above 320 C (fouling onset).
  • Bridgewall above 830 C.
  • Stack O2 outside 2 - 6 % band.
  • Excess air above 30 %.
  • Heat-balance vs process-side gap above 3 percentage points (instrument drift).

7. Pressure Convention

Fuel pressure is reported in kPa gauge. Absolute pressure = gauge + atmospheric (default 101.3 kPa at sea level). The dashboard does not use fuel pressure in the efficiency calculation - it is reported as an operational reference for burner-trim diagnosis. Fuel volumetric flow at standard conditions is derived from mass flow and average MW: V = m · 22.414 / MW · 3600 (Sm3/hr at 0 C, 101.3 kPa).

8. Series Conventions on Charts

Online series are continuous lines. Reference, design, and threshold lines are dashed. Dual-axis charts (where used) show the primary scale on the left, secondary on the right with no gridlines. Each chart line breaks at any timestamp where the underlying value is null, so a flat segment is real data and a gap is missing data.

9. Time Range Behavior

The topbar pill bar (1H, 24H, 7D, 30D, 90D) and the inline custom range (number + unit) drive both the trend chart x-axis and the Performance Averages KPI row. The Current Values row is unaffected by the range and always reflects the latest reading in the dataset.

The chart x-axis is padded to the full requested window. If the dataset is shorter than the window, the older portion shows as blank space rather than being compressed onto a shorter axis. The window cutoff is computed off the latest dataset timestamp, not off wall-clock time, so demo and historical data behave identically.

10. Data Quality & Limitations

The bundled demo dataset covers 60 daily aggregated rows interpolated backwards from a single client-supplied operating snapshot, with realistic per-channel noise applied. Fuel-composition GC samples are placed on a 3-day cadence rotating through Sheet Reference, Summer EOR, and Winter SOR cases. The latest row reproduces the snapshot exactly so the heat-balance efficiency closes at 83.64 %.

The two bridgewall thermocouples are averaged for the trend chart; the spread between them is normal cell-to-cell variation (typically under 5 C).

Fuel pressure is reported but does not enter the efficiency calculation. It is shown for operator diagnosis (burner pressure-flow relationship and tip fouling).

This is a daily-aggregated dashboard. The 1H and 24H pills resolve correctly but show the latest day on a finer grid; full sub-daily resolution requires a historian connection.

11. Common Fault Modes

Stack temperature creep over weeks, efficiency falling: convection-bundle fouling. Schedule offline cleaning. Verify with bridgewall temp (should also rise slightly as heat shifts from convection back to radiant).

Stack O2 drifting up, excess air rising, efficiency falling: burner air-register drift or damper opening creep. Check air register positions and FD damper modulation.

Stack O2 dropping below 2 %, CO sensor (if present) ramping: incomplete combustion. Trim air register up immediately. Risk of soot deposition and tube fire-side fouling.

Bridgewall climbing while stack temp normal: radiant-section tube fouling on the process side. Check process pressure drop and tube skin temperatures.

Heat-balance and PTC 4 efficiency agree but process-side disagrees: process flow meter or Cp_p error. Audit the meter calibration and confirm fluid composition.

Heat-balance and process-side agree but PTC 4 disagrees: ambient-temperature sensor drift, or radiation-loss assumption out of date for the heater. Recalibrate or update the loss percentage.
