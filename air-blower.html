Equipment

Dual-train centrifugal air blower (Blowers A and B), driven by a 3-phase induction motor. One train operates at a time; the other is on standby. The active train is detected automatically from motor current.

What this dashboard computes

For each timestamp on the active train: shaft power from electrical input, pressure ratio and dP across the machine, three efficiency methods (polytropic per ASME PTC 10 §5.4a, isentropic per §5.4, and fluid-power as a hydraulic indicator), max vibration across probes, and max bearing temperature across probes. Tiered alerts roll up to a single status (OK / Advisory / Alarm / Trip) per the open Manual.

Scope

Reference-grade proof of concept. Not a control system, not an SIS, not a substitute for a formal ASME PTC 10 acceptance test. Efficiency values are not clamped to 0-100 % - a value outside that range is a data-quality signal worth investigating.

Open the Manual button on the Dashboard tab for the full data contract, equation derivations, and operating envelope.

Engineering Manual - Air Blower

1. Purpose & Scope

This dashboard monitors performance and mechanical health of a dual-train centrifugal air blower (Blowers A / B, one active at a time) driven by a 3-phase induction motor. It computes shaft power, pressure ratio and dP, three efficiency metrics, max vibration, max bearing temperature, and a tiered alert roll-up. Inputs are timestamped historian rows; outputs are KPI tiles, trend charts, an alert log, and an exportable CSV.

Scope is reference-grade PoC. The dashboard is not a control system, not an SIS, and not a substitute for a formal ASME PTC 10 acceptance test. Fluid-power efficiency is an incompressible-flow hydraulic indicator only and is not a thermodynamically rigorous compressor efficiency.

2. Equipment Context

Centrifugal blower trains, single-stage, motor-driven through a direct or speed-increased coupling. Service is combustion air or process air at near-atmospheric suction. Suction is referenced as absolute (kPaa) because the suction-side instrumentation reads true absolute below atmospheric when the inlet filter is loaded. Discharge is referenced as gauge (kPag) because the discharge transmitter is plant-standard PI-tagged.

Vibration is monitored on up to 4 probes per train (typically two casing or bearing-housing points and two on the shaft or coupling area). Bearing temperature is monitored on up to 2 RTDs per train (drive end and non-drive end). Filter dP is monitored on the suction air filter to flag clogging. Bypass valve position is monitored to detect recycle and capacity-limit conditions.

5. Calculations

Shaft power. Standard 3-phase motor input power equation. P_elec = sqrt(3) · V · I · PF / 1000 in kW. Defaults V = 4000 V (motor datasheet), PF = 0.85 (nameplate). Reference: IEEE / NEMA motor basics.

Pressure normalization. Convert mixed-unit plant measurements to absolute bar. P1 = P_suct_kPaa / 100. P2 = P_disch_kPag / 100 + P_atm. dP = P2 - P1. r = P2 / P1. Site atmospheric pressure default P_atm = 0.93 bar for an Alberta installation; tune to site elevation.

Fluid-power indicator. P_fluid = Q · dP / 36 in kW, where Q is in Nm³/hr and dP is in bar. The constant 36 absorbs the unit conversion Nm³/hr · bar × 1e5 Pa per bar / (3600 s · 1e3 W per kW) = 1/36. Then n_fluid = P_fluid / P_elec · 100 %. This is an incompressible-flow hydraulic ratio, used as a trending indicator only. It is not a thermodynamically rigorous compressor efficiency. Use polytropic for cross-machine comparison.

Isentropic efficiency. Per ASME PTC 10 §5.4. With T in Kelvin: n_s = T1 · ((P2/P1)^((k-1)/k) - 1) / (T2 - T1) · 100 %. Air uses k = 1.40 from the ASHRAE Handbook of Fundamentals.

Polytropic efficiency (headline). Per ASME PTC 10 §5.4a and API 617. Define the polytropic temperature exponent sigma = ln(T2/T1) / ln(P2/P1). Then n_p = ((k-1)/k) / sigma · 100 %. For a centrifugal compressor with monotonic compression, n_p is always greater than or equal to n_s. Polytropic is independent of compression ratio, which makes it the right metric for cross-machine and degradation tracking.

6. Status Logic

Status is rolled up from independent checks and reported as a single overall pill on the banner: OK / Advisory / Alarm / Trip. Triple-encoded (color, dot, text) per ISA-101.

Vibration tier (ISO 10816-3 Group 1, large machines greater than 300 kW on rigid foundation). Advisory at Zone B/C boundary (default 4.5 mm/s). Alarm at C/D (7.1 mm/s). Trip at Zone D (11.0 mm/s, long-term operation not permissible). For flexible-foundation Group 1 or smaller-machine groups, retune the thresholds.

Bearing temperature tier. Manufacturer-typical for oil-lubricated journal bearings. Advisory 70 °C. Alarm 85 °C. Trip 95 °C. Adjust to the bearing datasheet.

Process advisories. Filter dP greater than 0.10 bar (replace filter). Blower dP above design max (default 1.00 bar at the dashboard, originally 0.45 bar in engine.py - tune to nameplate). Bypass valve greater than 60 % open (recirculating excess flow). Bypass closed below 5 % combined with discharge pressure below setpoint (machine at capacity limit).

7. Pressure Convention

Suction is absolute (kPaa) because the suction-side transmitter is a true absolute reference and reads partial vacuum when the inlet air filter is loaded. Discharge is gauge (kPag) because the discharge transmitter is plant-standard PI-tagged. Internally the engine normalizes both to absolute bar before computing dP, ratio, and the thermodynamic efficiencies. Site atmospheric pressure is a configurable setting (default 0.93 bar for an inland Alberta site at roughly 700 m elevation). For coastal or high-altitude sites, set the atmospheric pressure to match.

8. Series Conventions on Charts

All four trend charts share conventions. Solid line is the online value at each timestamp. Dashed line is a reference level (vibration advisory, bearing advisory, design dP). The right axis is reserved for the secondary metric in dual-axis charts (flow on the power chart, blower dP on the bearing chart). Series colors come from the Ferriq palette: #BB6E3D primary, #3A7D52 green, #C4890E amber, #763D16 dark gold. Status red #D4432A is reserved for alarm-threshold overlay lines.

9. Time Range Behavior

The pills in the topbar (1H, 24H, 7D, 30D, 90D) and the inline custom range (number plus sec/min/hr/day) drive both the trend chart x-axis and the Performance Averages KPI row. The Current Values row is unaffected and always shows the latest reading in the dataset. Cutoff math is computed off the latest timestamp in the dataset, not the wall clock - so historical CSVs and demo data behave the same way.

The chart x-axis pads to the full requested window. If the dataset is shorter than the window, older slots are null-filled and the line breaks (spanGaps: false). Selecting 90D with 45 days of data shows 45 days of blank space at the start, not a compressed 45-day chart. Restore Defaults sets the active pill back to 30D and clears the custom range inputs.

10. Data Quality & Limitations

Gas is assumed to be air (k = 1.40). Do not reuse this engine for process gases without updating the gamma setting. The motor power factor uses nameplate value (0.85); replace with measured PF if a recent test exists. Flow measurement is assumed accurate per ISO 5167 (orifice or venturi, typical ±2 % full scale); no uncertainty propagation in this PoC.

Suction temperature is forward-filled up to 4 hours when missing, then falls back to a fixed default of 4.0 °C. Discharge temperature is required for the thermodynamic efficiencies. If only one train has a discharge transmitter, the dashboard reports n_isentropic and n_polytropic as blank when the un-instrumented train is active and falls back to fluid-power as the headline. Vibration interpretation uses ISO 10816-3 Group 1; for flexible-foundation or smaller machines, retune the thresholds via the limits inputs.

11. Common Fault Modes

Bearing temperature degradation. Steady, monotonic climb in max bearing T over weeks while load is constant. Suggests lube-oil contamination, cooling-water side fouling, or seal wear. Demo data shows this signature - bearing T2 climbs from 54 °C (Dec 16) to 75 °C (Jan 28), crossing the 70 °C advisory mid-January.

Vibration uptick at constant load. Step or ramp increase in max vibration across one or more probes with no change in current or pressure. Suggests rotor unbalance, coupling wear, or impeller fouling.

Inlet filter clog. Filter dP climbs above 0.10 bar at constant flow. Suction pressure drops below the typical envelope. Replace filter.

Bypass-locked-open. Bypass position pinned greater than 60 % at non-startup conditions. Recycling flow, increasing energy cost. Investigate controller setpoint and bypass actuator.

Capacity limit. Bypass closed below 5 % and discharge pressure below setpoint. Machine cannot meet demand at current speed. Consider second train start or downstream demand reduction.

12. References

ASME PTC 10 - Performance Test Code on Compressors and Exhausters (isentropic and polytropic efficiency definitions, §5.4, §5.4a). API 617 - Axial and Centrifugal Compressors and Expander-Compressors (polytropic as primary). ISO 10816-3 - Mechanical vibration evaluation of machines, in-situ measurements (Group 1 zone boundaries B/C, C/D, D). ISO 5167 - Measurement of fluid flow by means of pressure differential devices (orifice plates, nozzles, venturi tubes). ASHRAE Handbook - Fundamentals (air properties, gamma value).
